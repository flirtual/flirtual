# Swedish (sv)

The Homie/Date terms are **provisional** until a native reviewer confirms them.

## Register

- Everywhere: **du** (Swedish has no formal register in practice). Ban and moderation notices
  are polite and calm.
- Legal pages: usual Swedish legal style, third person ("användaren").
