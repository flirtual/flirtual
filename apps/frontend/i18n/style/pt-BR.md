# Portuguese, Brazil (pt-BR)

Register and terms are **provisional** until a native reviewer confirms them.

## Register

- App, emails, push: **você**, friendly and casual.
- Ban, warning and moderation notices: você, polite and calm.
- Legal pages: usual Brazilian legal style, third person ("o Usuário"), no direct address.

## Style

- Brazilian vocabulary: usuário, tela, celular, cadastrar, arquivo, compartilhar.
- "Dates" in the dating sense is "encontros", never "datas" (calendar dates).
- Gender-neutral by rephrasing ("alguém", "pessoa"), not with @/x endings.

## Plurals

`one`, `other` (`many` is for millions and optional). Note `one` covers 0 and 1.
