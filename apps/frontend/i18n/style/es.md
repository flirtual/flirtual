# Spanish (es)

One translation for Spain and Latin America. Register and the Homie/Date terms are
**provisional** until a native reviewer confirms them.

## Register

- App, emails, push: **tú**, friendly and casual. Avoid vosotros and regional slang; prefer
  wording understood everywhere ("ustedes" for plural address if unavoidable).
- Ban, warning and moderation notices: **usted**, polite and calm.
- Legal pages: usual Spanish legal style, third person ("el usuario"), no direct address.

## Style

- Gender-neutral by rephrasing ("le gustas a alguien", "personas"), not with @/x/e endings.
- Opening ¡ and ¿ always.

## Plurals

`one`, `other` (`many` is for millions and optional).
