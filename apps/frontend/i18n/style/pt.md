# Portuguese, Portugal (pt)

European Portuguese, not Brazilian. Register and the Homie/Date terms are **provisional**
until a native reviewer confirms them.

## Register

- App, emails, push: **tu** (European usage), friendly and casual.
- Ban, warning and moderation notices: polite and calm, with **você**-free formal phrasing
  (impersonal constructions); never "o senhor".
- Legal pages: usual Portuguese legal style, third person ("o Utilizador"), no direct address.

## Style

- **Never "amigar"**: in Portugal it colloquially means moving in together as a couple.
- European vocabulary and spelling: utilizador (not usuário), ecrã (not tela), telemóvel
  (not celular), registar, ficheiro, partilhar.
- Gender-neutral by rephrasing ("fazer amizade", "alguém"), not with @/x endings.

## Plurals

`one`, `other` (`many` is for millions and optional).
