# French (fr)

Register and the Homie/Date terms are **provisional** until a native reviewer confirms them.

## Register

- App, emails, push: **tu**, friendly and casual.
- Ban, warning and moderation notices: **vous**, polite and calm.
- Legal pages: usual French legal style, third person ("l'utilisateur"), no direct address.

## Style

- **No inclusive writing** (no point médian: ami·e, connecté·e). Rephrase so no gender agreement
  is needed: use verbs with avoir and neutral nouns ("veut être pote avec toi", "quelqu’un t’a
  liké", "une personne"), not adjectives or participles that agree with the user.
- Typography: non-breaking space before ; : ! ? and inside « ».
- Write the apostrophe as ’ (U+2019), especially before `{placeholder}`: `l’{name}`. A straight
  ' before { breaks the placeholder.

## Plurals

`one`, `other` (`many` is for millions and optional). Note French `one` covers 0 and 1.
