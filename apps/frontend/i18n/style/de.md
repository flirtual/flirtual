# German (de)

Register and the Homie/Date terms are **provisional** until a native reviewer confirms them.

## Register

- App, emails, push: **du**, lowercase (du, dich, dein), friendly and casual.
- Ban, warning and moderation notices: **Sie**, polite and calm.
- Legal pages: usual German legal style, third person ("der Nutzer" / "die Nutzer"), no direct
  address.

## Terminology

| English | German |
|---|---|
| Homie (button) | Kumpel ("hat dich als Kumpel markiert") |
| Homie Mode | Kumpel-Modus |
| Date Mode | Dating-Modus |
| Like (button) | Like (provisional) |
| Premium | Premium |
| NSFW (feature) | NSFW |
| NSFW (disallowed) | Nacktheit oder explizite sexuelle Inhalte (provisional) |
| They/Them | they/them (English; no established German form) |
| She/Her, He/Him | sie/ihr, er/ihm |

## Style

- Avoid long noun compounds in buttons; prefer short verbs ("Speichern", "Weiter").
- Gender-neutral by rephrasing ("Wer dich geliked hat"), not with Gendersternchen or colons.
- Use „…" quotation marks.

## Plurals

`one`, `other`.
