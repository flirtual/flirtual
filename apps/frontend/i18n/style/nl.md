# Dutch (nl)

Register and the Homie/Date terms are **provisional** until a native reviewer confirms them.

## Register

- App, emails, push: **je/jij**, friendly and casual.
- Ban, warning and moderation notices: **u**, polite and calm.
- Legal pages: usual Dutch legal style, third person ("de gebruiker"), no direct address.
